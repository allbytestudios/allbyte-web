At the start of a New Game you choose a difficulty for the playthrough. Note you can't change it later so consider carefully. 

| | **Easy** | **Medium** | **Hard** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Enemies** | weaker | normal | stronger |
| **Respawns** | slow | normal | quick |
| **Skill tree** | no Expertise | with Expertise | with Expertise |
| **Gear, skills, & relics** | majority | almost all | all |

**What it means for how you play:**
- **Easy** leans into the story, little grinding, enemies are weaker, and skills stay simple and don't get stronger (no Expertise).
- **Medium** is the intended balance with full combat depth including the **Expertise** system (skills grow the more you use them) and includes a moderate grind.
- **Hard** is combat heavy. Stronger enemies pushing you to grind and strategise more (leaning on items, positioning, and build). But, you can find all equipment, relics, and skills on this difficulty.
