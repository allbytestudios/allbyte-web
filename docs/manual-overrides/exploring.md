Outside battle, explore the world of Nesis. There are many NPCs, items, and places to discover.

- **NPCs**: Many NPCs have lots of helpful information, and they have different things to say depending on what's happening across Nesis at the time.
- **Treasure appearances.** Chests, item bags, and relics appear across Nesis so be sure to explore all the nooks and crannies across each location.
- **Rest**: resting fully restores HP and MP. Villages often have inns you can pay to stay in, but there are some safe spaces in the wild as well; in **Laria** you can rest for free in the bed at **Elias' house**.
- **Save**: from the menu, you can save almost anywhere, including dungeons, *except* during certain scripted scene events. Be careful of saving from a difficult situation to back out of. Multiple saves might be helpful.
- **AutoSave** will save after every scene change and combat.
- **Scene-transition markers** *(on by default)*, a **floating animated triangle** hovers over every way *out* of an area, so you always see where you can leave. Toggle them with the **△** button.
- **Input-mode toast**: switching between input modes flashes a small icon top-right showing which mode you're in.
