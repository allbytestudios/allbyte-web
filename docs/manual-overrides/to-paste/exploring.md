Outside battle you explore Nesis. Talking to people and looking around will teach you a great deal about the world, and turn up powerful items to help you along the way. NPC dialogue changes as the story progresses, so a person with nothing to offer today may be worth finding again later.

## Treasure
![Chest and bag](obj_chest_bag) Chests and bags holding useful items can be found across the world of Nesis.

![Relic](obj_relic) Relics can be found in dungeons or towns. Each one holds both a story of the past and a combat mechanic.

![Combat drop](obj_drop) Enemies have a chance to drop items during combat. Walk over the dropped chest before an enemy does or the end of combat to secure it.

## Resting
![Bed](obj_bed) A bed restores HP and MP in full. Some places charge to rest, others do not. Elias' house in Laria is free.

## Saving
![Auto-Saved toast](obj_save) You can save from the menu almost anywhere, dungeons included, so long as you are not inside an event or a fight.

The game also keeps one auto-save of its own, overwritten after you change area, win a fight, or move the story forward. It waits until you have control again, so it never lands mid-scene.

## Scene Exits
![Exit marker](obj_exit) An exit marker hovers over every way out of an area, so you can always see where you can leave. They are on by default; toggle them with ![E](btn_e) ![O](btn_o) ![Triangle](btn_tri) outside battle.

## Controller Icons
![Controller icons](obj_modes) Controller icons appear to indicate which controller mode the game is in: keyboard, mouse, gamepad, or touch (see **Controls** for details).
