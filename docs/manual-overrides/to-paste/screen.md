**The six regions:**
1. **Turn order** *(left rail)*, who acts next, in **initiative** order. It reads **bottom-up**: the unit acting now is at the bottom, the next above it.
2. **Your unit panel** *(bottom-left)*, the active character's **portrait, name, and Level**, plus **HP** and **MP**. (Full breakdown below.)
3. **Action menu** *(bottom-centre)*, your turn's choices: **Attack · Move · Skill · End · Run**.
4. **Selected action** *(highlighted in the menu)*, the action you have chosen.
5. **Terrain panel** *(left of the menu)*, the cell under the cursor and what is on it.
6. **The grid** *(on the field)*, the tiles the fight is fought on, laid over the ground you were standing on. The bracket marks the cell under your cursor, the one region 5 describes.

A **targeted enemy's** panel appears when you aim at one. It shows the same fields,
with detail only if you have the **Scan** relic; otherwise **???**.

## Combat log
The **combat log** shows each attack's hit chance and result, **HIT / MISS / CRIT** and the damage, so you can see *why* a fight swung the way it did. Toggle it with the on-screen **LOG** button, ![R](btn_r) on a keyboard, or ![L1](btn_l1) on a controller.
