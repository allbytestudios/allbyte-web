You begin as **[Elias]**, in his room in the remote village of **[Laria]**. Before you head out, learn the
room — three things matter here:

- **The bed** — right where you woke. You can rest here to restore your health and magic. Remember it: after
  your first real fight, this is where we'll come back to recover.
- **The treasure chest** — beside the bed. Open it for **[100 Sen]**, your starting money. (A looted chest
  shows as **open**, so at a glance you can tell which ones you've already emptied.)
- **The door** — top-left. That's the way out.

With the chest emptied, head for the door.
