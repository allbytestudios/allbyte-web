In the morning, see **[Mayor Raeges]** one last time before you head out. He sends you to the **[Waterways]**
beneath Laria, the real source of the trouble, and ending it falls to you.

This is the last stop before the dungeon. Before you dive in, it's worth taking care of a few things around
town, the scenes that follow cover the pickups and the shop run, and end with a short checklist to get you
ready for the Waterways.

→ When you're prepped, the way down is through **[Aremis' Windmill]** → **[basement]** → into the
**[Waterways]**.
