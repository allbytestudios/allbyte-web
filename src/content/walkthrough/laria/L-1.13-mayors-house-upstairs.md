**Optional pickup.** While you're in the Mayor's house, head **upstairs** — the chest there holds
**[Valerian Powder] x3**, which **cures poison** (handy against the poison-slinging enemies in the Waterways).

(You may notice a **[Health Relic]** referenced on the Mayor's floors, but it's out of reach in normal play —
don't waste time hunting for it.)

--> Back downstairs and out to **[Main Square]**.
