**Optional, but strongly recommended before the dungeon.** The General Store sits on **[Front Road]**. Talk
to the **[Merchant]** to open the shop — this is your one good chance to gear up.

On sale:

- **[Mugwort]** — **heals HP**. This is your *only* HP-restoring item, so stock up on several.
- **[Irid Liquor]** — **restores MP** (fuel for skills like Smite and Cure).
- **[Valerian Powder]** — **cures poison** (the Waterways enemies poison you).
- **[Leather Chestpiece]** and **[Leather Boots]** — your first real armor. Glenn's Longsword handles your
  attack; armor keeps you standing.

(There's a chest in the store too, but it's out of reach in normal play — ignore it.)

---

### Before you dive into the Waterways

The way down is through **[Aremis' Windmill]** → **[basement]** → the **[Waterways]**. Once you step through
that passage, you're in the dungeon. Get ready first:

- **Rest in Elias' bed** so you go in at full HP/MP — especially after the fight at Church Square.
- **Buy a few [Mugwort]** here at the General Store (and any **[Leather]** armor you can afford).
- **Save your game** before you dive in.

When you're topped up, armored, and saved — head to the Waterways.

--> Down into **[The Waterways]**.
