From Aremis' Windmill basement you drop into the **Waterways** — the flooded channels beneath Laria, and the
real source of the slime trouble. Work your way down through the channels toward the sluice gate at the bottom.

**Grab the [Scan Relic]** you'll find in the channels — a relic you can slot to read enemy information in
battle.

### Enemies

**Slime**
- **HP:** 13 · **Attack:** melee, range 1 (weak) · **Weakness:** none — physical works fine
- The same gentle foe from town. A hit or two drops it.

**VSlime** *(the real threat)*
- **HP:** 13 · **Attack:** melee, range 1 · faster than a Slime (higher agility)
- **Halves physical damage** (physical resist) and shrugs off magic too (high magic defense) — slow to kill by
  either type.
- **Poison trail:** leaves a **persistent poison patch** on tiles it crosses (it lasts the whole fight). Don't
  stand in it and don't chase the VSlime through it. It also **absorbs poison** (heals from it), so poison won't
  work on it.
- Pick VSlimes off one at a time, keep your spacing, and mind where the trails are.

*(Slot the **[Scan Relic]** to read any enemy's stats mid-battle — otherwise the enemy stat block shows "???".)*

This is a good place to **level up** before the boss. The channels are full of slimes, and a couple of levels
make the fight at the bottom much safer. Heal between fights with **[Mugwort]**, and retreat to recover if a
fight goes badly.

--> Fight your way down to the **[Sluice Gate]** at the end of the Waterways.
