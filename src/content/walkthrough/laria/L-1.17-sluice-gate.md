At the bottom of the Waterways sits the **Sluice Gate** — and behind the whole infestation, the **Mother
Slime**. This is the boss of Laria.

### Boss — Mother Slime

She's far tougher than the trash slimes and **halves physical damage**, so Glenn's Longsword alone will be a
grind — lean on any skills or magic you have, and be patient. She's **immune to poison**, so don't count on
that. Keep your health topped up with **[Mugwort]**, don't let her corner you, and chip her down steadily.
Defeating her drops the **[Berserckounter Relic]**.

If the fight turns against you, back off into the Waterways, grind a level or two on the slimes, and return
stronger.

--> With the Mother Slime dead, the infestation is over. Head back to **[Mayor Raeges]** in Laria to report —
and to learn what comes next.
