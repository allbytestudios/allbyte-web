At the bottom of the Waterways sits the **Sluice Gate** — and behind the whole infestation, the **Mother
Slime**. This is the boss of Laria.

### Boss — Mother Slime

**HP:** 28 · **Size:** large (2-tile footprint)

**Attacks & range:**
- **Melee** (range 1) — hits hard; keep your health above one solid blow.
- **Poison Cloud** — a **radius-3 area** of magic damage + poison, centered on her, cast every few turns
  (**more often on harder difficulty**). This is the danger: she doesn't need to be next to you. You have to be
  *adjacent (range 1)* to hit her, but the cloud reaches **3 tiles out** — so you can't fully dodge it while
  attacking. Cure poison with **[Valerian Powder]** and keep MP up with **[Irid Liquor]** for your skills.
- **Splits** — every hit she **survives** spawns an add (Slime or VSlime). A **killing blow spawns nothing**, so
  the swarm only grows if you chip her slowly — another reason to hit hard.

**Strategy:**
- **Physical is a trap.** Her physical resist + defense **halve** sword damage (~2 per hit → a long grind that
  also spawns adds). **Radiant [Smite] bypasses her resistance** and is the intended answer — if you invested the
  off-stats (WIS/INT) to power it, Smite hits *hard* here and can end her fast (fewer hits = fewer adds).
- **You can still win without Smite** — just level up more in the Waterways first, bring plenty of **[Mugwort]**
  (your only HP heal) + **[Valerian Powder]** for the cloud's poison, and out-last her. It's slower and messier
  (more adds), but doable.
- **Manage the adds** — kill her *fast* to avoid the swarm; if adds pile up, thin the VSlimes (watch their poison
  trails) before returning to her.
- **If it turns against you,** retreat into the Waterways, grind a level or two, restock at the store, and return.
- Defeating her drops the **[Berserckounter Relic]**.
