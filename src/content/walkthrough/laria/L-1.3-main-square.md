Main Square is the heart of Laria and the hub the whole village hangs off — the windmills, the shops, the
Mayor's house all branch from here. This is where you'll pass through again and again.

For now, our path runs south: take **[Front Road]** toward **[Aremis' Windmill]** to answer Naphtali's
errand.

There are a couple of worthwhile stops around the square too — a chest upstairs in the Mayor's house, and one
in **[Igna's Windmill]** — but they're optional. We'll cover them in **Other Scenes**; for the main story,
keep moving toward the windmill.

--> Take **[Front Road]** south.
