Down in the basement, the water intake feeds the mill. Two things here:

- **Grab the [Mugwort]** growing on the far left — a healing herb, and you'll want it soon.
- **Walk onto the lever** by the intake. That's when you find the culprit: a **slime** has clogged the works.
  You've found the problem — but you don't fight it yet.

Notice the passage out of this basement: it leads down into the **Waterways**, the dungeon under Laria.
**Don't go in yet** — the Mayor needs to hear about this first, and you're not ready for what's down there.

--> Head back up and out to **[Front Road]**, then return to the **[Mayor's House]** in Main Square.
