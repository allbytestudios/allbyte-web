Head back into Church Square. As you arrive, a scene starts on its own — slimes are attacking a villager, and
Elias needs something to fight with.

Partway through the scene, you're asked to try something and control comes back to you.

> **TO PROGRESS:** **Run in any direction.** That's the whole input — the scene is waiting on movement, not on
> a dialogue advance or an interact. Nothing on screen says so, and standing still leaves you stuck mid-scene.

The scene plays out the rest — Elias takes up **[Glenn's Longsword]** — and leads straight into your first fight.

### First Battle — Slimes

Combat is turn-based on a tile grid. On your turn, pick an action:
- **Move** — step across tiles toward an enemy (costs AP).
- **Attack** — strike an enemy you're standing next to (melee is range 1).
- **Ability** — use a skill (you have none yet).
- **End** — end your turn.
- **Run Away** — flee the battle.

So: **Move** next to a slime, then **Attack**. With Glenn's Longsword they drop in a hit or two — clear them all.
(You can't use items in battle yet — that needs the **Item Relic**, which comes later.)
