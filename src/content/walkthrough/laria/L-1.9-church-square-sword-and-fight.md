Head back into Church Square. As you arrive, a scene starts on its own — slimes are attacking a villager, and
Elias needs something to fight with.

Partway through the scene, you're asked to try something — just **run in any direction** to continue. The scene
plays out the rest — Elias takes up **[Glenn's Longsword]** — and leads straight into your first fight.

### First Battle — Slimes

This is your first fight, and it's a gentle one. A few things to learn:

- **Move and attack on the grid.** Step Elias next to a slime and strike. With Glenn's Longsword, the town
  slimes fall in a hit or two.
- **Watch your health.** If you take a knock, open the menu and use a **[Mugwort]** to heal.
- **You earn experience** for the win — your first steps toward leveling up.

Clear the slimes and the woman is safe.
